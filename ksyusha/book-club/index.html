Words to learn

Read all the phrases and examples first. Try saying each example out loud. You'll need them in every task.

The story

It's a real page-turner.I read it in two evenings. It's a real page-turner.
The plot is gripping.The plot is gripping from the very first chapter.
It's fast-paced / slow-paced.It's quite slow-paced at first, but then it speeds up.
There's a plot twist.There's a huge plot twist in the middle.
It's a bit predictable.I guessed the ending. It's a bit predictable.

The characters

The characters feel real.The characters feel real, like people I know.
I can relate to…I can relate to Red. She's going through a lot.
I can't stand…I can't stand characters who lie all the time.
It's told from … point of view.It's told from Red's point of view.
The main character is…The main character is an 18-year-old girl.

How it makes you feel

It keeps you on the edge of your seat.Every chapter keeps you on the edge of your seat.
I couldn't put it down.I couldn't put it down until 2 a.m.
It's a bit creepy.Reading it at night is a bit creepy.
It didn't grab me.The first book didn't grab me, so I stopped.
It stayed with me.The ending stayed with me for days.

Your opinion

Personally, …Personally, I prefer thrillers to romance.
If you ask me, …If you ask me, the cover is the best part.
The way I see it, …The way I see it, a good book needs good characters.
As far as I'm concerned, …As far as I'm concerned, long books are worth it.

Two sides

On the one hand, … On the other hand, …On the one hand, it's exciting. On the other hand, it's very dark.
That said, …It's slow. That said, the ending is great.
Then again, …I don't like YA books. Then again, this one is different.
Even so, …I guessed the twist. Even so, I enjoyed it.

Adding a point

What's more, …The plot is clever. What's more, it's funny.
On top of that, …It's too long. On top of that, the ending is weak.
Not only that, but…Not only that, but the characters are great too.
For instance, …Some scenes are scary. For instance, the first night.

Being honest

To be honest, …To be honest, I skipped a few pages.
I might be wrong, but…I might be wrong, but I think Simon knows something.
The thing is, …The thing is, I don't trust anyone in this book.
I'm not sure, but…I'm not sure, but I think it's based on a true story.

Guessing

My guess is that…My guess is that the secret is about money.
I have a feeling that…I have a feeling that Maddy is hiding something.
I bet…I bet there's a big twist at the end.
It wouldn't surprise me if…It wouldn't surprise me if Arthur knew more.
