Be or Do?

When do we use am / is / are / was / were, and when do we need do / does / did?

Start here

Two families

Read this first. Come back here if you get stuck.

The BE family

now: am / is / are · past: was / were
  • Negative: add not after it. is → isn’t, was → wasn’t
  • Question: put it first. She is → Is she…?
  • Short answer: Yes, she is. / No, it wasn’t.

The DO family (all other verbs)

now: do / does · past: did
  • Negative: don’t / doesn’t / didn’t + verb. She doesn’t work.
  • Question: Do / Does / Did + who + verb? Did you go?
  • After do / does / did the verb is simple: work, go (not works, went)
Step 1: find the verb. Is it am / is / are / was / were? Yes → BE No → DO
