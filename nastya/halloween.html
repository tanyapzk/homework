Reported Speech Guide

Tenses, Time, & Questions
Tenses: Shift One Step Back
Present Simple → Past Simple
"I like tea." → She said she liked tea.
Present Cont. → Past Cont.
"I'm working." → He said he was working.
Present Perfect → Past Perfect
"I've finished." → She said she had finished.
Past Simple → Past Perfect
"I saw Tom." → He said he had seen Tom.
will → would
"I'll call." → She said she would call.
can / must → could / had to
"I can go." → He said he could go.
Time & Place Changes
now→then
today→that day
tonight→that night
yesterday→the day before
tomorrow→the next day
last week→the week before
here / this→there / that
said: She said (that) she was busy.
told: She told me (that) she was busy.
No Change Required
Reporting verb in Present: "I'm tired." → She says she is tired.
Modal verbs (would, could, should, might): "I could help." → He said he could help.
Past Perfect: "I had left." → She said she had left.
Universal truths / facts: "Water boils at 100°C." → He said water boils at 100°C.
Reported Questions
Yes / No Questions

Use asked + if / whether + normal statement word order.
"Do you like coffee?" → She asked if he liked coffee.

Wh- Questions

Use asked + what / where / when + normal statement word order.
"Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived (no auxiliary do/did).
